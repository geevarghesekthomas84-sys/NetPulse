NetPulse – Interactive Network Protocol Visualizer & Online Simulator

Understand how the internet truly works. Explore 39 networking protocols with step-by-step packet animations, inspect raw bitfields in a Wireshark-grade dissector, and build, test, and troubleshoot enterprise networks with realistic hardware.

Supported Networking Protocols

HTTP/1.1, HTTP/2, HTTP/3 QUIC, DNS, DHCP, TCP 3-Way Handshake, UDP, IPv4, IPv6, ICMP Ping, ARP, OSPF, BGP, VLAN 802.1Q, Spanning Tree Protocol (STP), TLS 1.3, SSH, WebSocket, and WPA3.
